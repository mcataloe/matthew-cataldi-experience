# DIN Modernization Proof of Concept

## Context

A Department of State modernization team evaluated cloud and hybrid-connectivity approaches for isolated or regulated environments across AWS, Azure, GCP, Equinix, Terraform, and migration-discovery tooling.

The verified stage was proof of concept and test/integration. The work did not become a production migration or adopted program during Matthew's engagement.

## Matthew's contribution

Matthew served as lead engineer and senior individual contributor, materially directing architecture and technical decisions while informally guiding engineers.

He:

- Presented recommendations directly to customers and contributed estimates, budget recommendations, acceptance criteria, and feasibility analysis while final approval remained customer-owned.
- Wrote Terraform modules/configuration, ran plans and applies, and created infrastructure in proof-of-concept and test/integration environments.
- Created and configured AWS and GCP resources/projects for the modernization proof of concept.
- Designed Azure resources and connectivity while the Azure team created subscriptions and resources because of tenant-ownership boundaries.
- Designed, configured, and tested AWS Direct Connect, Azure ExpressRoute, and Equinix Fabric approaches in proof-of-concept/test environments.
- Designed the use of Equinix Network Edge without claiming device creation or primary configuration.
- Installed and configured Stratozone scanners, initiated and troubleshot discovery, analyzed results, and refined cloud-sizing and migration estimates.
- Identified that Stratozone port-scanning behavior could resemble attack traffic and trigger on-premises security controls, creating a material discovery and migration-planning risk.
- Implemented and demonstrated a persisted proof of concept across Terraform, multi-cloud infrastructure, hybrid connectivity, and discovery tooling.

## Technical environment

AWS, Azure, GCP, Terraform, AWS Direct Connect, Azure ExpressRoute, Equinix Fabric, Equinix Network Edge design, Stratozone, isolated-environment discovery, migration assessment, and customer demonstrations.
