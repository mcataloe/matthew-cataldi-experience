# Engineering Analytics and Build-versus-Buy Evaluation

## Context

An enterprise modernization organization was evaluating how to aggregate engineering-delivery and operational metadata across systems such as GitLab, GitHub, Azure DevOps, Jira, ServiceNow, and Confluence.

## Matthew's contribution

Matthew served as the early technical lead and primary architectural driver.

He:

- Established initial platform direction and integration strategy.
- Developed an Express.js backend API.
- Defined ingestion, normalization, extensibility, and reporting concepts.
- Identified scalability, maintainability, ownership, and extensibility risks in heavily customized low-code approaches.
- Conducted build-versus-buy analysis comparing internal development with commercial engineering-intelligence platforms.
- Evaluated DevEx, DORA, developer productivity, operational health, implementation effort, data freshness, cost, latency, integration depth, adoption, and long-term ownership.
- Worked with security and compliance stakeholders to bound ingestion around operational metadata rather than source code or sensitive descriptive content.
- Produced executive-level comparisons and phased adoption recommendations.
- Mentored and coordinated supporting contributors.

## Technical environment

Express.js, JavaScript/TypeScript, GitLab, GitHub, Azure DevOps, Jira, ServiceNow, Confluence, engineering telemetry, operational metadata, analytics pipelines, DevEx concepts, and DORA-oriented measurement.

## Evidence boundaries

- Matthew led early architecture and evaluation; the repository does not claim that he delivered a complete enterprise analytics platform into mature production operation.
- Vendor evaluation is evidence of platform-analysis depth, not implementation ownership of the commercial products evaluated.
