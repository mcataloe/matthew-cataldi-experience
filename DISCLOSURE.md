# Disclosure and Interpretation Policy

## Purpose

This repository is a curated public projection of a private career Source of Truth. Its purpose is to provide useful professional evidence without publishing personal contact information, raw job-search material, or unnecessary operational details.

## Included information

The repository may include:

- Employers, role titles, and approximate dates
- Publicly defensible project descriptions
- Technologies and architecture patterns
- Documented leadership and contribution scope
- Certifications and education
- Technical capabilities supported by project evidence
- Explicit limitations that prevent overstatement

## Excluded information

The repository intentionally excludes:

- Home location, phone number, and personal email address
- Raw intake interviews and working notes
- Recruiter communications and interview preparation
- Compensation targets and negotiation strategy
- Personal employment-search constraints beyond role positioning
- Names of individual colleagues
- Credentials, secrets, internal URLs, source code, or customer data
- Non-public implementation details that are unnecessary to establish professional capability
- Claims still awaiting confirmation or disclosure review

## Government and regulated work

Descriptions of federal, healthcare, and regulated projects are intentionally bounded. They describe professional responsibilities and generally applicable engineering patterns without providing credentials, sensitive data, or a detailed map of a customer environment.

An omission should not be interpreted as lack of experience. It may reflect confidentiality, disclosure limits, or the decision that additional detail is not necessary for public evaluation.

## Contribution boundaries

Reviewers and AI assistants should preserve the verbs used in this repository:

- `led` does not necessarily mean sole ownership;
- `supported` does not mean led;
- `evaluated` does not mean implemented;
- `participated` does not imply decision authority;
- `designed` does not automatically mean operated in production;
- technology listed in a project should be interpreted in the context of the documented contribution.

## Verification

This repository is informational. Employers should use normal interview, reference, background-check, and credential-verification processes for hiring decisions.

## Reuse

No broad license is granted for republishing the repository as a separate work. Linking, quoting limited portions for candidate evaluation, and using the content to assess Matthew's professional experience are the intended uses.
