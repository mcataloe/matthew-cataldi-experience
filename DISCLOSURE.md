# Disclosure and Interpretation Policy

## Purpose

This repository is a curated professional projection of a private career Source of Truth. It is intended for public sharing, but the repository's current GitHub visibility setting should be checked separately before relying on that intent.

Its purpose is to provide useful professional evidence without publishing personal contact information, raw job-search material, private source records, or unnecessary operational details.

## Authority

The private career Source of Truth remains authoritative. This repository, its structured claim index, project pages, capability pages, and comprehensive CV are downstream publication artifacts.

A generated file or repository commit does not become factual authority merely because it is public, versioned, comprehensive, or convenient to query.

## Included information

The repository may include:

- Employers, official role titles, and approved dates
- Publicly defensible project descriptions
- Technologies and architecture patterns at documented contribution depth
- Documented leadership, implementation, operational, advisory, and evaluation scope
- Certifications, clearance wording, and education approved for public display
- Technical capabilities supported by project evidence
- Natural limitations needed to prevent overstatement
- A comprehensive public career CV compiled from currently approved records

## Excluded information

The repository intentionally excludes:

- Phone number, personal email, home address, and other direct PII
- Raw Intake interviews, checkpoints, evidence inventories, and working notes
- Recruiter communications, interview preparation, compensation targets, and private strategy
- Names of individual colleagues and private stakeholders
- Credentials, secrets, internal URLs, source code, customer data, and security-sensitive topology
- Non-public implementation detail unnecessary to establish professional capability
- Claims still awaiting confirmation, scope resolution, evidence, or disclosure review
- Provisional claims while the configured publication policy has `allow_provisional_claims: false`
- The private Source of Truth repository name or URL in target-local public metadata

## Coverage omissions

An omission should not automatically be interpreted as lack of experience. It may reflect confidentiality, disclosure limits, incomplete Source of Truth coverage, or a decision that the detail is not necessary for public evaluation.

The July 2026 comprehensive CV intentionally omits Associated Global Systems, non-XW100 Sysmex material, and deeper Rockford technical-platform detail because those scopes are not currently approved for publication under the no-provisional-claims policy.

## Government and regulated work

Descriptions of federal, healthcare, and regulated projects are intentionally bounded. They describe professional responsibilities and generally applicable engineering patterns without exposing credentials, sensitive data, individual colleagues, or a detailed map of a customer environment.

Project stage must be preserved. Test/integration, proof-of-concept, non-production, production, and attributed post-transition outcomes are not interchangeable.

## Contribution boundaries

Reviewers and AI assistants should preserve the verbs and stages used in this repository:

- `led` does not necessarily mean sole ownership;
- `implemented` does not imply implementation of every listed system component;
- `operated` applies only to the documented system and environment scope;
- `supported` does not mean led;
- `evaluated` does not mean implemented;
- `participated` does not imply decision authority;
- `designed` does not automatically mean deployed or operated in production;
- `prototype`, `proof of concept`, `test/integration`, `non-production`, and `production` must remain distinct;
- a later attributed project outcome does not become Matthew's implementation ownership;
- technology listed in a project must be interpreted in the context of the documented contribution.

## AI-assisted work

AI-assisted code or architecture may be attributed to Matthew only where the documented boundary supports his review, testing, modification as needed, and acceptance of the implementation. AI-assisted development does not convert designed or roadmap features into working product capabilities.

## Verification

This repository is informational. Employers should use normal interview, reference, background-check, and credential-verification processes for hiring decisions.

## Reuse

No broad license is granted for republishing the repository as a separate work. Linking, quoting limited portions for candidate evaluation, and using the content to assess Matthew's professional experience are the intended uses.
